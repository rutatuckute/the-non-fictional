What the seven posts actually contain
Centred body copy
Every post centres its paragraphs with an inline style="text-align: center" — 2 to 8 per piece, 29 in total. A ragged left edge removes the anchor the eye returns to on each line. It lives in the markdown, not the stylesheet, so fixing it means either overriding it in CSS or editing seven files.
Excerpts run long
215 to 381 characters, full sentences. Longer than a standfirst usually is, which suits an article page and means the card grid has to clamp rather than stretch.
Section headings are mis-taxonomised
Four of seven posts class their <h4> as general-theory or current-affairs while their category_id is essays — so headings take a red or green from a taxonomy the filters no longer use. Below they take the form colour instead.
Emphasis is hard-coded
Inline <span style="color:#ff5c5c"> marks emphasis, and quotes carry style="color: white". Both bypass the token set, so neither follows a theme or a form colour.
